Property Management Services

Managing rental properties can be time-consuming and complex, especially for owners who juggle multiple responsibilities or live out of state. iGate Realty LLC provides comprehensive property management services designed to safeguard your investment and ensure predictable, stable returns.

Our management solutions include rent collection, property maintenance coordination, tenant relations, inspections, lease renewals, and legal compliance. We handle requests and issues promptly, ensuring that tenants feel supported and your property remains in excellent condition.

Through transparent financial reporting and regular updates, we keep property owners fully informed of income, expenses, and ongoing maintenance. Our priority is to maximize your rental income while minimizing your workload, giving you peace of mind and consistent profitability.

What We Do:

  • Rent collection & financial reporting
  • 24/7 maintenance coordination
  • Inspections & property upkeep
  • Lease enforcement & renewals
  • Owner statements & expense tracking

Our goal is to maximize your returns while minimizing your involvement.

  • Strong tenant communication and fast issue resolution
  • Automated rent collection & transparent financial statements
  • Compliance with local Indiana leasing laws and regulations
  • Preventive maintenance to protect long-term property value
