Residential Property Buying & Selling

Buying or selling a home is one of the most significant decisions in a person’s life, and iGate Realty LLC provides the expertise and personalized support to make the entire process smooth, informed, and rewarding.

For homebuyers, we begin by understanding your lifestyle, budget, and preferences, then curate properties that match your vision—whether it’s a starter home, family home, or a luxury residence. We offer neighborhood insights, school district information, market trends, and guidance on inspections, financing, and closing.

For sellers, we position your property for maximum visibility and value. Our team performs a detailed market analysis, recommends pricing strategies, and enhances property appeal through professional listing photos, presentation guidance, and targeted marketing. From negotiating offers to managing paperwork and closing formalities, we ensure a seamless, stress-free experience that results in the best possible outcome for your transaction.

Our team guides you through every stage, including property search, pricing strategy, marketing, negotiations, inspections, and closing support. Whether you're a first-time buyer or an experienced homeowner, we ensure you get the best value and a smooth experience from start to finish.
