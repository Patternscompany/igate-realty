Why Dubai?

  • Tax-friendly & investor-friendly environment: Dubai offers favorable tax conditions with many tax-free benefits on rental income and capital gains (depending on home jurisdiction).
  • High demand, strong rental yields & solid appreciation: Dubai continues to outperform many global markets in rental ROI and resale value.
  • Strategic international hub: Business, tourism, expatriate influx, and global connectivity drive year-round real estate demand.
  • Foreign ownership allowed: Non-GCC buyers can own property outright in designated freehold zones.
  • Portfolio diversification: Adding Dubai real estate spreads geographic, currency, and regulatory risk—ideal for global investors.

Dubai offers a stable, high-growth, globally accessible real-estate market that attracts international investors—aligning perfectly with iGate Realty’s mission of global property expansion.
