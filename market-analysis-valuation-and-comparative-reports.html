Market Analysis, Valuation & Comparative Reports

Accurate data is the foundation of smart real estate decisions, and iGate Realty LLC delivers clear, comprehensive reports that help buyers, sellers, and investors understand true property values and market dynamics.

Our analyses include comparative market evaluations, pricing assessments, rental rate benchmarking, neighborhood trends, school district performance, and future market projections.

For sellers, we identify the optimal listing price to attract buyers while maximizing returns. For buyers, we assess property values to ensure you make well-informed offers. For investors, we provide market-driven insights into cash flow potential, occupancy rates, and long-term appreciation.

These reports give our clients confidence, clarity, and an edge in the competitive real estate landscape.

Our Reports Include:

  • Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)
  • Rental market assessments
  • Pricing recommendations
  • Investment performance projections
  • Neighborhood & school district insights

These insights ensure you buy, sell, or invest with confidence.
