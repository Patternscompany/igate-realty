Know the True Value of Your Property

Whether you're buying, selling, investing, or refinancing, understanding a property’s true market value is essential. iGate Realty provides detailed market analysis, professional valuations, and data-backed comparative market reports (CMAs) across Chesterton, Porter County, Valparaiso, Portage, Michigan City, and surrounding areas.

Our reports are built using current market trends, neighborhood evaluations, recent sales data, rental insights, and expert forecasting—helping you make confident and profitable decisions.

What’s Included in Our Market & Valuation Reports

✔ Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)

A detailed comparison of similar properties recently sold, currently listed, or expired in your area.

✔ Neighborhood Evaluation

School ratings, amenities, crime statistics, rental rates, market appreciation, and livability scores.

✔ Property Condition Review

Structural condition, interior/exterior assessment, upgrades, and depreciation factors.

✔ Pricing Strategy Recommendations

Suggested listing price, offer price, rental estimate, and projected return on investment (ROI).

✔ Supply & Demand Metrics

Days on market, buyer demand levels, seasonal trends, and predicted shifts in market activity.

✔ 12-Month Forecasting

Price appreciation, rental growth rate, and future investment potential in your neighborhood.
