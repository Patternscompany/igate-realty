Rental Homes & Leasing

Whether you're a tenant searching for a comfortable and reliable rental home or a landlord seeking quality tenants, iGate Realty LLC delivers a streamlined and dependable leasing experience.

We maintain a wide selection of rental homes in Chesterton and surrounding areas, offering tenants well-maintained properties that fit their needs and budget. Our process includes guided tours, clear communication of lease terms, and assistance with applications and move-in procedures.

For landlords, we handle everything from marketing vacant units to conducting thorough tenant screenings—including background checks, employment verification, rental history reviews, and credit evaluation. Our goal is to minimize vacancy periods, secure responsible tenants, and maintain compliance with local leasing laws. We ensure your rental property remains profitable and well-managed while protecting your interests as a property owner.

  • Lease preparation
  • Rental market evaluation
  • Reliable communication with tenants & owners
  • Efficient vacancy management
