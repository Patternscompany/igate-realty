Our Home Selling Strategy

  1. Home Evaluation & Pricing Strategy
    We analyze comparable sales, market trends, and your property’s condition to recommend the ideal listing price.
  2. Preparing Your Home for Market
    Guidance on repairs, staging, decluttering, curb appeal, and improvements to boost value.
  3. Professional Photography & Marketing
    High-quality photos, 360° virtual tours, videos, and strong descriptions to highlight your home’s best features.
  4. Maximum Visibility
    Your home is promoted on MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, social media, Google ads, email campaigns, and our buyer network.
  5. Showings & Open Houses
    We manage all buyer showings, screen leads, and host open houses professionally.
  6. Offers & Negotiations
    We negotiate aggressively to secure the best possible price and terms.
  7. Inspection, Appraisal & Closing
    We coordinate inspections, handle contingencies, and work closely with attorneys/title companies to finalize your sale.
