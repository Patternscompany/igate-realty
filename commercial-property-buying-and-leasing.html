Commercial Property Buying & Leasing

Businesses depend on the right location to operate efficiently, attract customers, and grow. iGate Realty LLC assists companies, entrepreneurs, and investors in finding commercial properties that align with business objectives—whether it’s office space, retail units, industrial warehouses, mixed-use buildings, or commercial land.

We analyze traffic flow, accessibility, zoning, infrastructure, and market viability to ensure you choose the ideal location. Our services cover site visits, negotiations, lease structuring, due diligence, and coordination with legal and financial teams.

For commercial investors, we provide occupancy insights, cap rate analysis, and long-term income projections. Whether expanding, relocating, or launching a new venture, we support businesses with strategic real estate solutions that promote stability, efficiency, and growth.

Our Commercial Real Estate Process

  • 1. Business Needs Assessment: Understanding goals, space type, customer base & budget.
  • 2. Market Research & Site Selection: Identifying high-potential commercial zones.
  • 3. Property Tours & Evaluation: Reviewing suitability, visibility & operational fit.
  • 4. Negotiation & Structuring: Handling lease terms, pricing & contract conditions.
  • 5. Due Diligence: Zoning checks, inspections & compliance verification.
  • 6. Closing Support: Coordinating with attorneys, lenders & title companies.
  • 7. Post-Closing Support: Optional leasing, management & tenant coordination.
